# Gotit

Copy on your phone, paste on your PC, and the other way round.

Gotit shares what you copy between an Android phone and a Windows PC on the same Wi-Fi. Copied text and images cross over by themselves; files go when you send them. The two devices talk directly to each other, encrypted, with no server and no account.

Status: early work. The plan and the design are in [docs/PLAN.md](docs/PLAN.md) and [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

- Android 10 or later, and Windows 10 or 11.
- Free, open source, no ads, no tracking.
- Installs come from this repo's Releases page, and both apps update themselves.
